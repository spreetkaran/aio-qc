Your first five minutes

  1. Open a video — Ctrl+O, drag a file onto the window, or use the arrow on the Load Video button for recent files.
  2. Play with Space. Step frames with , and . or the mouse wheel; jump with ← / → by the step chosen under the video.
  3. Choose the output folder with the Output Dir button, and the image format, quality and file names on the toolbar's output bar.
  4. Mark frames with M — it works while playing. Markers appear on the lane above the timeline strip.
  5. Extract the marked frames with Ctrl+E — or press Ctrl+S to save just the current frame.
Tip — Help ▸ Get Started replays the interactive tour at any time.

Where things are saved

  • Frames: the output folder, named by your naming template (default {video}_frame_{frame}).
  • Per-video session (bookmarks, in/out points, ROI, markers): restored automatically the next time you open the video.
  • Settings, logs, caches: AIO QC's settings and data folder (and its one log file) — see Settings & performance.
