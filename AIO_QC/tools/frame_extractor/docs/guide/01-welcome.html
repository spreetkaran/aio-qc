Welcome to the Frame Extractor

The Frame Extractor — AIO QC's video mode — turns hours of video into a clean, annotation-ready image dataset — and then tells you how well your detection models really perform on that footage.

What you can do

Play anything, exactly
Smooth real-time playback up to 8K and 60+ fps, frame-exact stepping and seeking, original-quality zoom to the pixel, timecode in every format.		 Pick the right frames
Drop markers while the video plays, snap them to the sharpest frame, let the timeline suggest scene starts or the sharpest frame per window, crop to a region of interest.
Extract at scale
Batch-process many videos in parallel with blur, duplicate and scene filters, cross-video de-duplication, model-based mining and pre-annotations — interrupt and resume any time.		 QC the frames
Send what you extracted straight to Image QC (Tools ▸ Open output in Image QC) to review labels, run Model Check, and measure models in Image QC ▸ Models ▸ Testing.
Find moments
Search every frame of your videos by description (“white van at night”) or by example.		 Share results
Export lossless or frame-exact clips and annotated videos with every model's detections.

The typical workflow

  1. Open a video (Ctrl+O or drag it onto the window).
  2. Pick frames with markers (M), or set up a batch job for many videos (Ctrl+B).
  3. Review the extracted frames and reject the bad ones (Ctrl+G).
  4. Annotate in your labelling tool — the Frame Extractor can pre-annotate with your models.
  5. Evaluate new model weights in Image QC ▸ Models ▸ Testing (Ctrl+Alt+L) and share the report.
