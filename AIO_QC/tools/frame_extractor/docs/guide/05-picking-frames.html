Picking frames

Markers — the fastest way to pick

Press M to drop a marker at the playhead — also while the video plays. Markers live on the lane above the timeline strip and in the Markers dock.

Marker colours: green = pending, blue = saved, hollow = suggested.

Bookmarks and in / out points

  • B toggles a bookmark (with an optional note in the Marks dock); Ctrl+← / → jumps between them.
  • I / O set the in and out points; Shift+I / O go there; X clears them. In / out limit batch jobs, clip export and Testing video analysis.

Region of interest (ROI)

R enters ROI mode: drag to draw; drag inside to move; drag edges or corners to resize. Shift+R clears it. Saved frames are cropped to the ROI (use Save Full, Ctrl+Shift+S, to ignore it). T makes the ROI follow an object while playing or stepping.

The timeline strip

A background pass analyses the video (about two samples per second, coarse to fine): blue bars show sharpness (red below your blur threshold), the orange line shows motion, white ticks mark scene cuts. Right-click it to set the blur threshold from the current frame, add a model track, or re-analyse. F / Shift+F jump to the next / previous flagged sample.

Detections on the picture

D shows your models' detections on the paused frame (configure models in Settings ▸ Models). Each model has its own colour — the same colour it has in Testing and in reports.
